Eligald Industrial Chemicals Limited is a Nairobi-based supplier of industrial solvents, acids, bases, and specialty chemicals, serving manufacturers, processors, laboratories, and water-treatment authorities across Kenya and the wider East African Community.

From our warehouse in Imara Daima, we supply a curated range spanning 16 product categories — from agrochemicals and construction additives to laboratory reagents and water-treatment solutions. Every product we handle is sourced from vetted manufacturers, with full Safety Data Sheets and Certificates of Analysis available on request.

We combine technical know-how with dependable logistics — helping businesses across Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Burundi, South Sudan, DR Congo, and Somalia get the right chemicals, correctly documented, when they need them.
