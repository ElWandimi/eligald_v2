Ordering & Minimum Quantities

Ordering & Minimum Quantities

Most products are available from 25 kg / 25 L jerry can quantities upward, with better pricing at drum (200 kg/L) and IBC/bulk tank volumes. Smaller trial quantities can be arranged for first-time buyers testing a new chemical — message us with the product and quantity you need.

We primarily serve businesses — manufacturers, processors, and distributors — so our standard packaging starts at 25 kg/L. We don't sell in consumer retail pack sizes.

Delivery & Coverage

Delivery & Coverage

We supply across the East African Community: Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Burundi, South Sudan, DR Congo, and Somalia, dispatched from our Nairobi warehouse.

Nairobi and surrounding counties: typically 1–2 business days. Rest of Kenya: 2–4 business days. Regional (Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, etc.): typically 5–10 business days depending on border clearance and destination. Exact timelines are confirmed when you place an order.

Yes — for regional orders we provide the commercial invoice, packing list, and any chemical-specific export documentation needed for customs clearance in the destination country.

Payment & Pricing

Payment & Pricing

Most industrial chemical pricing varies with quantity, grade, and market conditions, so we quote per enquiry rather than listing fixed prices — message us via WhatsApp or the enquiry form with the product and quantity for a same-day quote.

We accept M-Pesa, Airtel Money, and direct bank transfer. Payment details are shared with your quote once the order is confirmed.

Orders are paid in full before dispatch. This keeps pricing and lead times predictable for every customer, new or repeat.
